03 / WHAT IS PARTNERSHIP MASU

What is Partnership Masu?

FOMUSglobal Partnership Masu is a special cultural artifact crafted annually by FOMUS.

This Masu is not merely a craft object. It symbolizes the cultural direction FOMUS envisions for that year, and the very stance of co-creation.

This product is offered as a physical 'Masu' sale, and a one-year partnership begins from the date your purchase is completed.

During the partnership period, there may be opportunities for priority information sharing and dialogue regarding FOMUS's initiatives related to Japanese culture.

However, these are not rights or guarantees. They are possibilities for relationships that emerge naturally through context, alignment of philosophy, and timing.

The Masu is specially crafted and will be stored in Japan or shipped within 1-3 months.
