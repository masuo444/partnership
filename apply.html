INTRODUCTION

Please read before applying

FOMUS GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP is neither a luxury tour nor an experience-based service.

This partnership is a relationship to nurture trust over time with the custodians of Japanese culture, within unseen workshops, philosophies, and connections, and to create something together.

This is not suitable for those seeking immediate returns or predetermined experience menus.

However, for those who:

• Want to see Japanese culture as a 'relationship' rather than 'consumption'
• Want ongoing involvement, not a one-time experience
• Are ready to enter the cultural sphere of FOMUS

This will be a very quiet, profound entrance.

WHO

Who you connect with

FOMUS GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP is a direct partnership with MaSU, the founder of FOMUS.

MaSU has traveled across Japan, building long-term relationships with artisans, brewers, and craftspeople. He is a practitioner who has connected culture and business in approximately 20 countries, using the traditional 'Masu' craft as his axis.

Workshops that remain hidden. Philosophies left unspoken. Sites shared only within trust. He has worked as a bridge connecting these 'inner' worlds with people, companies, and decision-makers around the globe.

What you encounter through this partnership is not a 'guided Japan.' It is a Japan that welcomes you through trust.

About the Partnership Agreement

FOMUS GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP is not a service agreement. It is a partnership agreement based on mutual trust. Only those who confirm and agree to the following may proceed.

  • This agreement does not guarantee outcomes or experiences
  • Respect for confidential information and relationships is required
  • Participation may be terminated if trust is breached
  • The contract period is one year

【FOMUS GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP Agreement Terms】

Article 1 (Purpose)
This agreement defines the terms for participation in FOMUS GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP (hereinafter 'the Partnership').

Article 2 (Nature of Agreement)
This agreement does not guarantee specific outcomes, experiences, or results. The Partnership aims to build ongoing relationships based on mutual trust.

Article 3 (Confidentiality)
Participants shall not disclose confidential information, personal information of related parties, or internal process information obtained through the Partnership to third parties.

Article 4 (Maintaining Trust)
Participants are obligated to maintain trust with FOMUS, its associates, and other partners. FOMUS reserves the right to terminate participation if trust-damaging behavior is identified.

Article 5 (Contract Period)
This agreement is valid for one year from the date of execution.

Article 6 (Refunds)
Due to the nature of the Partnership, refunds are generally not provided.

Article 7 (Governing Law)
This agreement shall be governed by Japanese law.
